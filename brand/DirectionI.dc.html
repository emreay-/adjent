The offset pair knocked out of a solid tile and cropped hard by its edges, so the lines have no ends — they read as a detail of something larger, which is what a monitor is looking at. A filled tile is a much larger colour signal than a stroke, so the verdict is readable in peripheral vision — but it is also the loudest thing in the tray.
Tradeoff
A hard-edged filled square is exactly the app-icon plate the OS wants to supply itself, and it will look wrong beside macOS and Windows icons that are all rounded. It is also the noisiest thing in a tray designed around silence — worth checking whether an outline plate carries the idea without shouting.
