Marks not chosen
05 · alternates, each with its 16px cut
B — Pace ticks
A closed ring cut by the two marks that matter in the chart: now, and the pace line. Reads as an instrument face rather than a progress bar.
Tradeoff: the ticks touch the ring at 16px and it silts up into a blob. It also cannot animate with utilization, so the tray would need a second, unrelated shape.
C — Consumed
A solid disc with the spent wedge cut out. The most legible of the three at any size, and unmistakably about a quantity being used up.
Tradeoff: a filled pie is heavy in a tray of thin system glyphs, and it inverts the app's own drawing — the shipped tray fills a ring, not a disc.
D — Nested windows
Three concentric arcs for the three things stacked at once: session, weekly, per-model. The only mark here that says many limits rather than one.
Tradeoff: it argues against the product's own editorial rule. Adjent shows one hero number; a mark that promises three is a promise the panel deliberately does not keep.
