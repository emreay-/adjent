One radius, one reason: launcher grids on macOS and most Linux desktops mask every icon to a rounded shape, and a square plate there reads as a mistake. Everywhere the app controls its own edge — tray, header, print, web — the plate stays hard. Never a circle, never a border, never a drop shadow.
