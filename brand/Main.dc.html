01 · the mark
adjent
how much, against how much you should have
The mark is the instrument, not a picture of one. Two columns stand on a common foot: the left is filled to what you have burned, the right is marked at the pace line — where that number would be if the window were spent evenly. The difference between them is the entire product. It is drawn once, in rows and columns on a pixel grid, and the 16px tray glyph is the same drawing rather than a reduction of it.
