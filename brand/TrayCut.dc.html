The tray cut
03 · 16px is its own design
A plate with two knockouts is three features in 256 pixels, and rasterising it down produced a coloured blob with dirt on it. So the tray glyph is a reduction, not a miniature: figure and ground invert, the plate goes, and the two rules become the mark. It is drawn on the pixel grid with hard edges — a 1:2 slope steps exactly one pixel across for every two down, which reads as a line where an antialiased diagonal reads as a smear.

Rule & wall is the most distinctive silhouette here — a diagonal against a vertical reads as designed rather than as punctuation — but the wall is not in the logo, so the tray would carry a shape the brand never uses.

Single slab is the boldest and the calmest, and it cannot show drift at all: utilization would have to live entirely in the colour, which the panel's own rules forbid.
